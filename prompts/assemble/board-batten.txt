BOARD AND BATTEN shutter. Three to four vertical boards of equal width butted edge to edge, held together by two horizontal battens (one near the top, one near the bottom) fastened across the face; on tall panels a third batten at mid-height. The battens sit proud of the boards, casting a small shadow. Board joints read as fine vertical lines. Square top and bottom, no Z-brace, no arch, no cut-outs. Rustic but clean, a factory finish rather than rough lumber.
