RAISED PANEL shutter. A rectangular frame of stiles and rails with a solid raised panel in each section: a flat central field with a bevelled edge stepping up from a recessed border. Multi-section panels have a horizontal mid-rail, the panels equal in height or the upper slightly shorter. Stile width about one tenth of panel width. Surfaces are flat and solid — no louvers, no cut-outs, no ornament — with the only relief being the panel bevel, which catches light along its upper edge and shades below.
