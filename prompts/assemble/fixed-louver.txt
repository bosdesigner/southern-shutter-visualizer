FIXED LOUVER shutter. A rectangular frame of stiles and rails with fixed, downward-sloping horizontal louvers filling each section; the louvers are evenly spaced, slightly overlapping, and set at a consistent angle so their top edge faces the house. A horizontal mid-rail divides multi-section panels; sections are equal or the upper slightly shorter. Stile width about one tenth of panel width. No raised panels, no cut-outs, no visible hardware except two small hinge points at the outer stile. Louvers read as crisp, individual slats — never a flat panel with painted lines.
