MOVABLE (OPERABLE) LOUVER shutter. A rectangular frame of stiles and rails with operable horizontal louvers in each section, set at a uniform slightly-open angle so thin lines of shadow show between slats, and a vertical tilt rod running down the centre of each louvered section connecting the louvers. The tilt rod is the identifying detail — a slender vertical bar on the face of the louvers. Multi-section panels have a horizontal mid-rail with one tilt rod per section. Stile width about one tenth of panel width. No raised panels, no cut-outs.
