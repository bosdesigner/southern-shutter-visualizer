BAHAMA (BERMUDA) shutter. ONE louvered panel per window, as wide as the whole window opening, hinged along its TOP edge above the window and propped out from the wall at the bottom at about a 45-degree angle on two slender telescoping arms, so the panel shades the window like an awning. Horizontal louvers fill the panel within a frame of stiles and rails. The underside of the panel is visible from the street and is the same color as the face. Nothing is mounted beside the windows — no side shutters at all.
