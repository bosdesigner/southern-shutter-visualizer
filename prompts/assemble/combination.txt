COMBINATION shutter. A rectangular frame of stiles and rails with a raised solid panel in the LOWER section and fixed downward-sloping louvers in the UPPER section, divided by a horizontal mid-rail. The louvered section is the taller of the two on tall windows. Stile width about one tenth of panel width. Louvers are crisp individual slats; the raised panel has a bevelled edge. No cut-outs, no ornament, no visible hardware except two small hinge points at the outer stile.
